# Customer Segmentation — SQL Results

These three CSV files are exported from the PostgreSQL business analysis queries in [Customer_Segmentation.sql](../Customer_Segmentation.sql).

| Result File | Business Question |
|---|---|
| [customer_segment_performance.csv](customer_segment_performance.csv) | Which segments contribute the most customers and positive sales? |
| [rfm_segment_behavior.csv](rfm_segment_behavior.csv) | How do average recency, purchase frequency and spending differ by segment? |
| [high_value_at_risk_customers.csv](high_value_at_risk_customers.csv) | Who are the ten highest-spending customers within the At Risk segment? |

**Scope:** 4,338 eligible customers. Sales are positive purchases and exclude cancellations/refunds. The dataset is historical (2010–2011).
